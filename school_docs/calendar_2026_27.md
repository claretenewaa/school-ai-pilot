# Academic Calendar 2026/2027
Madina Bright Future Academy (SAMPLE CALENDAR FOR THE AI PILOT. Dates are to be confirmed by the school and may change if GES announces a different calendar.)

## Term 1: 8 September 2026 to 18 December 2026
- Tuesday 8 September 2026: school reopens. Fees due by Friday 11 September.
- Monday 21 September 2026: Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Day, public holiday (no school).
- Saturday 10 October 2026: PTA meeting at 9:00 am.
- Week of 12 October 2026: mid-term class tests.
- Thursday 22 and Friday 23 October 2026: mid-term break (no school).
- Friday 30 October 2026: deadline for BECE registration details (JHS 3).
- Friday 4 December 2026: Farmers' Day, public holiday (no school).
- Monday 7 December to Tuesday 15 December 2026: end-of-term examinations.
- Thursday 17 December 2026: Christmas party and Speech and Prize-Giving Day.
- Friday 18 December 2026: school vacates; report cards collected by parents.

## Term 2: 12 January 2027 to 25 March 2027
- Tuesday 12 January 2027: school reopens.
- Saturday 6 February 2027: PTA meeting at 9:00 am.
- Week of 8 February 2027: first mock examination for JHS 3 and mid-term class tests for other classes.
- Thursday 18 and Friday 19 February 2027: mid-term break (no school).
- Saturday 6 March 2027: Independence Day. Monday 8 March 2027 is a holiday if the government declares it.
- Around Wednesday 10 March 2027: Eid al-Fitr, public holiday (date depends on the moon sighting).
- Monday 15 March to Tuesday 23 March 2027: end-of-term examinations.
- Thursday 25 March 2027: school vacates; report cards collected.
- Friday 26 March 2027: Good Friday. Monday 29 March 2027: Easter Monday.

## Term 3: 20 April 2027 to 23 July 2027
- Tuesday 20 April 2027: school reopens.
- Week of 26 April 2027: second mock examination for JHS 3.
- Saturday 1 May 2027: May Day.
- Around Monday 17 May 2027: Eid al-Adha, public holiday (date depends on the moon sighting).
- May or June 2027: BECE for JHS 3, on dates set by WAEC.
- Saturday 22 May 2027: PTA meeting at 9:00 am.
- Thursday 10 and Friday 11 June 2027: mid-term break (no school).
- Thursday 1 July 2027: Republic Day, public holiday.
- Monday 12 July to Tuesday 20 July 2027: end-of-year examinations.
- Friday 23 July 2027: school vacates; promotion reports collected.

## Daily timing reminder
School starts at 7:30 am. Pick-up is by 5:00 pm.
