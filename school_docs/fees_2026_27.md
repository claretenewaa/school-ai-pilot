## Tuition fees per term
| Class | Tuition per term |
|---|---|
| Class 1 to Class 3 | GHS 1,350 |
| Class 4 to Class 6 | GHS 1,450 |
| JHS 1 and JHS 2 | GHS 1,650 |
| JHS 3 | GHS 1,850 (includes two mock examinations) |

Tuition covers teaching, class tests, examinations, report cards and the ICT lab.

## Other fees
- Admission fee (new learners only, once): GHS 300, non-refundable.
- PTA dues: GHS 50 per term.
- Books and exercise books (paid once a year, in Term 1): GHS 350 for Class 1 to Class 6, GHS 450 for JHS.
- Uniform set: GHS 220. PE kit: GHS 120. Ghanaian wear shirt: GHS 90.
- Feeding (optional): GHS 900 per term, or GHS 80 per week.
- After-school classes (optional, Class 4 to JHS 2): GHS 200 per term. JHS 3 extra classes: GHS 250 per term.
- French club (optional): GHS 150 per term.
- Late pick-up after 5:00 pm: GHS 20 per day.

## How to pay
- Mobile Money: pay to the school's MTN MoMo merchant number printed on your fee invoice. Use your child's student ID as the reference.
- Bank: pay into the school's bank account shown on the fee invoice, and send a photo of the pay-in slip to the office WhatsApp number.
- Cash: at the bursar's office, Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 3:00 pm. Always collect an official receipt.

## Payment deadlines and instalments
- Fees should be paid in full by the end of the first week of each term.
- Parents may pay in two instalments: at least 60% by the end of week 1 and the balance by the mid-term break.
- Parents who are having difficulty paying should speak to the bursar to agree a written payment plan. The school prefers a payment plan to sending a child home.
- Learners are never sent home for fees during class tests or examinations.
- Report cards for the term are released only when the term's fees are fully paid or a payment plan is in place.

## Discounts
- Sibling discount: 10% off tuition for the second child and 15% off for the third and later children.
- Early payment discount: 5% off tuition if the full year's tuition is paid before the first day of Term 1.
- Staff children: 50% off tuition.

## Refunds
- Tuition is not refunded after the second week of term.
- If a learner leaves before the term starts, tuition is refunded minus the admission fee.
- Bus and feeding fees for full unused weeks can be refunded if the office is told in writing one week ahead.
