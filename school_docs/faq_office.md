# Frequently Asked Questions from Parents
Madina Bright Future Academy (SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR THE AI PILOT)

## My child is sick. What should I do?
Keep the child at home and inform the class teacher or the office before 8:00 am by phone or WhatsApp. If the illness lasts three days or more, bring a hospital or clinic note when the child returns.

## Can my child write an exam they missed?
Yes, if the absence was due to illness and you provide a hospital note. A make-up paper is written within one week of the missed paper.

## How do I know how my child is doing before the report card?
A mid-term progress report is sent home in week 7. You can also book a meeting with the class teacher through the office at any time. During the AI pilot, teachers may also share a short progress summary.

## Can I change my child's bus pick-up point?
Yes. Inform the office in writing at least one week before. The fee may change if the new point is in a different zone.

## What happens if I cannot pay the full fees on time?
Speak to the bursar to agree a written payment plan. You can pay in two instalments: at least 60% in week 1 and the balance by mid-term.

## Does the school offer scholarships?
The school offers a limited number of needs-based bursaries each year, awarded by the board. Applications open in June for the next academic year. Ask the office for the form.

## How can I help my child with Mathematics at home?
Check and sign the homework book every evening, practise times tables and mental sums with your child, use everyday examples like counting change at the market, and talk to the Mathematics teacher about the topics your child finds difficult.

## Who do I contact about bullying?
Report it immediately to the class teacher or the headteacher. Every report is investigated, and both families are contacted.

## Can my child bring a phone or tablet?
No. Phones are not allowed in school. A phone found will be kept at the office and returned only to a parent.

## Is there a Saturday class?
Only for JHS 3 learners preparing for the BECE, from January, 8:00 am to 12:00 noon.
