## About the school
Madina Bright Future Academy is a private basic school in Madina serving Class 1 to JHS 3. We follow the Ghana Education Service (GES) Standards-Based Curriculum for Class 1 to Class 6 and the Common Core Programme for JHS 1 to JHS 3. Each class has one stream of about 24 learners. The Ghanaian language taught is Twi. French is offered as an after-school club.

## School hours
- The school gate opens at 6:30 am. Learners should arrive by 7:15 am.
- Morning assembly starts at 7:30 am on Mondays and Fridays; other days start with class devotion at 7:30 am.
- Lessons run from 7:45 am to 2:30 pm for Class 1 to Class 3, and from 7:45 am to 3:00 pm for Class 4 to JHS 3.
- Break time is 9:50 am to 10:20 am; lunch is 12:20 pm to 12:50 pm.
- After-school classes (optional) run from 3:00 pm to 4:30 pm, Monday to Thursday.
- Children must be picked up by 5:00 pm. After 5:00 pm a late pick-up fee of GHS 20 per day applies.

## Admissions
- Admission is open all year, subject to space. New learners write a short placement test in English and Mathematics.
- Documents needed: birth certificate or Ghana Card number of the child, last report card, transfer letter from the previous school (for transfers), two passport photographs, and the completed admission form.
- The admission fee is non-refundable. See the fee schedule for amounts.

## Attendance and punctuality
- Learners are expected to attend every school day. Our target is at least 95% attendance for every learner.
- If your child will be absent, call or send a WhatsApp message to the class teacher or the school office before 8:00 am, giving the reason.
- After an absence, send a note or message explaining it. For illness of three days or more, please bring a hospital or clinic note.
- A learner who arrives after 7:30 am is marked late. Three late arrivals in one week lead to a phone call to the parent.
- If a learner is absent for three days in a row without explanation, the class teacher will call the parent. After five days the headteacher will invite the parent for a meeting.
- Learners may only leave school early when a parent or an authorised guardian signs them out at the office.
- Family trips during term time are discouraged, especially in examination weeks.

## Uniform and appearance
- Class 1 to Class 6: yellow and brown checked dress (girls) or brown shorts with a yellow checked shirt (boys), brown socks and black shoes.
- JHS: brown skirt or trousers with a cream shirt, and the school tie.
- Wednesday is Ghanaian wear day: learners wear the school's kente-print shirt.
- PE kit (house T-shirt and shorts) is worn on PE days only.
- Hair must be kept short and neat. Girls may wear simple cornrows without extensions. No jewellery except small stud earrings for girls.
- Uniforms are sold at the school office.

## Homework
- Class 1 to Class 3: about 20 minutes of homework per day.
- Class 4 to Class 6: about 40 minutes per day.
- JHS: about 1 hour per day, more in JHS 3 before the BECE.
- Homework is written in the homework book, which parents should check and sign every evening.
- Homework submission is part of the continuous assessment (class score).

## Assessment, examinations and grading
- Each term has class tests, quizzes, homework, class exercises and an end-of-term examination.
- The class score (continuous assessment) counts for 30% of the term result and the end-of-term examination counts for 70%.
- Class 1 to Class 6 are graded with the Standards-Based Curriculum proficiency levels: 80 to 100 Highly Proficient (HP); 68 to 79 Proficient (P); 54 to 67 Approaching Proficiency (AP); 40 to 53 Developing (D); 39 and below Emerging (E).
- JHS learners are graded 1 to 9: 80 to 100 Grade 1 (Highest); 70 to 79 Grade 2 (Higher); 60 to 69 Grade 3 (High); 55 to 59 Grade 4 (High Average); 50 to 54 Grade 5 (Average); 45 to 49 Grade 6 (Low Average); 40 to 44 Grade 7 (Low); 35 to 39 Grade 8 (Lower); 0 to 34 Grade 9 (Lowest).
- A learner who misses an end-of-term examination for a medical reason, with a hospital note, can write a make-up paper within one week.
- Learners are not sent home for unpaid fees during class tests or examinations.

## Report cards and parent-teacher meetings
- Report cards are given out on the last day of each term. Parents must collect them in person or by an authorised guardian.
- A mid-term progress report is sent home in week 7 of every term.
- Parent-teacher conferences are held twice a year (October and February). Parents can also book a meeting with a teacher at any time through the office.
- Report cards show each subject's class score, exam score, total, grade or proficiency level, position in subject, and the teacher's remarks.

## Promotion and repetition
- Learners are promoted at the end of the academic year based on the three terms' work.
- A learner who scores below 40% average in both English Language and Mathematics may be advised to repeat the class. This decision is made by the headteacher after a meeting with the parents.

## BECE (JHS 3)
- JHS 3 learners write the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE), conducted by WAEC, usually in May or June.
- The school registers candidates in the first term of JHS 3. Parents must provide the child's birth certificate and correct spelling of names by the end of October.
- JHS 3 learners write two mock examinations: one in February and one in April.
- Extra classes for JHS 3 run Monday to Friday, 3:00 pm to 4:30 pm, and on Saturdays from 8:00 am to 12:00 noon from January.
- Choice of senior high school (SHS) is done with parents during the second term, guided by the school counsellor.

## Discipline and behaviour
- We use positive discipline. Corporal punishment (caning) is not used, in line with GES policy.
- Serious misconduct includes bullying, fighting, stealing, cheating in examinations, and bringing phones to school without permission.
- Consequences include a warning, a letter to parents, a parent meeting, detention after school, and, for very serious cases, suspension decided by the headteacher.
- Mobile phones are not allowed in school. A phone found will be kept at the office and returned only to a parent.

## Health and safety
- Inform the school of any allergies, asthma, sickle cell disease or other medical condition at admission.
- A sick learner will be kept in the sick bay and the parent will be called to pick the child up.
- The school does not give medicine without written permission from the parent.
- Please keep children with fever, vomiting, diarrhoea or chickenpox at home until they are well.
- The school is fenced and the gate is manned by a security officer from 6:00 am to 6:00 pm.

## Feeding and canteen
- Hot lunch is served daily for learners on the feeding plan (jollof, waakye, banku with okro soup, rice with stew, red red and fried plantain, among others).
- Parents may also send a packed lunch. Fizzy drinks and sweets are discouraged.
- The feeding fee is paid per term or per week (see the fee schedule).

## School bus
- The school bus covers Madina, Adenta, Ashongman, Haatso, Oyarifa and Ayi Mensah.
- Bus fees depend on the zone (see the fee schedule).
- Parents must be at the pick-up point on time; the bus waits for at most 5 minutes.

## Parent Teacher Association (PTA)
- All parents are members of the PTA. PTA meetings are held once a term on a Saturday at 9:00 am.
- PTA dues are paid every term with school fees.

- Student data is used only by the school and is never sold. Parents can ask the office to see the data held on their child.
